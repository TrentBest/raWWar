# raWWar — Player Roles

This document records roles already described by the creator. It is not a final class system.

## Soldier

The soldier is the foundational player role.

Possible activities include training, qualifying on equipment, accepting missions, deploying with a squad, marching with the unit, combat, downtime, recreation, and specialized work.

## Officer / Commander

Commanders operate from a first-person command environment.

They oversee staff and advisors, direct research, design bases, arrange facilities and training capacity, commission military reviews, address troops, make strategic decisions, and potentially enter combat personally.

## Cooperative participant

Cooperative play allows multiple players to inhabit complementary roles within the same campaign.

The design should avoid making every cooperative player a clone of the same combat role.

## Researcher

Researchers operate laboratories and conduct experiments.

The player may physically perform procedural tasks and record findings.

## Mechanic

Mechanics repair damaged equipment and vehicles through physical interaction.

## Construction squad

Construction players transport and assemble structural components.

## Logistics / Transport

Players can move materials such as construction plates to where they are needed.

## Pilot

Pilots operate aircraft.

## Spacecraft crew

Players can occupy weapons stations, tactical displays, helm positions, command positions, and other ship systems.

## Fleet command

Players can command ships and fleets from inside the command structure of a vessel.

## Recreation

Downtime supports social play and diegetic games such as arcade machines and simulated air hockey.

The important design rule is that these roles should feel like occupations inside the world, not menu selections that teleport the player into isolated mini-games.
