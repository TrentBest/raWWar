# raWWar — Multiplayer Design

Status: Initial design.

## Scale
Cooperative campaign should eventually support substantially larger groups than conventional small-party games.

The current target concept allows approximately 2 to 128 participating players in cooperative campaign scenarios.

## Cooperative does not mean identical
Players can cooperate within a shared campaign without necessarily belonging to the same faction.

Different players may have:
- different objectives;
- different chains of command;
- different qualifications;
- different information;
- different relationships;
- conflicting interests.

## Shared world
Players should inhabit the same systemic world rather than being placed into isolated copies of the same mini-game.

## Persistent multiplayer
Persistent modes extend this idea into worlds that continue beyond individual sessions.

Players can enter an ongoing universe and contribute to its history.
