## Executable evidence and current limits

Use [Examples](../examples/README.md), the [contract tests](../tests/raWWar.ContractTests/README.md), and the [production plan](PRODUCTION_PLAN.md) to distinguish implemented behavior from design intent. CI's current build and contract checks are tracked in [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/TrentBest/raWWar/actions/workflows/build.yml).

The living SVGs in this gallery communicate a world that should feel active. Their animation is not a simulation loop. Do not infer a hosted playable build, persistent world runtime, GPU rendering, or measured scale from these illustrations alone.
