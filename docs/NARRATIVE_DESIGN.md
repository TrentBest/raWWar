# raWWar — Narrative Design

Status: Living narrative document.

## Narrative philosophy
The story of raWWar should be experienced through participation in the world rather than through a constant stream of exposition.

The player is a person inside the war.

## Campaign opening
The opening sequence is established in the Game Design Bible and should be treated as the first narrative lesson.

Lightning, the palace, the living square, the soldiers, the order, the faction headquarters, the opportunity to explore, and the consequences of disobedience establish tone before the player is given a conventional tutorial.

## Factions
Major factions represent balancing powers opposing the Empress.

Each major faction should have its own physical presence, dogma, literature, recruiting materials, and culture.

Faction identity is not yet fully defined.

## The Empress
The Empress is established as a major authority figure who summons the Commander immediately at the beginning of the campaign.

Her history, relationship with the player, political structure, motivations, and the reason for the summons remain open.

## Environmental storytelling
The world should communicate history through:
- architecture;
- uniforms;
- equipment;
- insignia;
- propaganda;
- literature;
- conversations;
- damage;
- repairs;
- ceremonies;
- procedures;
- personal possessions.

## Ambient dialogue
Background conversations should include both consequential and mundane material.

Potential subjects include food, bathrooms, living quarters, equipment problems, relationships, jokes, rumors, complaints, assignments, politics, and recent events.

Not every conversation should be intended for the player.

## Player agency
Major narrative moments should preserve agency whenever practical. The headquarters assault is an established example: confrontation, conversation, and the final decision should belong to the player rather than being a predetermined cinematic.

## Narrative unknowns
The war, factions, player history, broader setting, campaign arc, victory conditions, and major characters remain under active elicitation.
