# Storage Facility

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** Physical inventory storage

A storage facility is keyed to what it stores: raw resource, processed material, ammunition, missiles, spare parts, fuel/energy, food, medical supplies or vehicle components.

Storage has finite capacity and does not create supplies.

If missile storage is empty, a missile tank cannot be resupplied from that storage.

The player should be able to establish an intent such as **keep forward anti-air batteries supplied**. Logistics seeks inventory and transport. The player does not need to manually order every crate unless deeper intervention is desired.

Re-keying uses the Candidate rule of 1/4 normal construction time and 2× normal construction cost. Exact inventory-transfer rules remain Illumination Needed.
