# Research Facility

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** Physical research capability

A research facility is a real place where people conduct experiments.

It requires the physical facility, an available administrative office in the underground HQ, qualified researchers, research equipment, power/resources, maintenance and a defined research program.

## Player interaction

The Commander physically visits the administrator. The administrator explains available research, prerequisites, expected benefits, constraints, staffing and demonstrations.

The player schedules a demonstration and can visit the facility to see the work. The resulting decision can be presented through a physical tablet or similar in-world device.

## Deterministic research

Research programs are defined. The player allocates capacity from 0–100. 100% means maximum throughput, not instant completion. Research reaches 100 deterministically and may unlock another defined program.

## Re-keying

A compatible facility may be re-keyed to another research domain using the Candidate rule of 1/4 normal construction time and 2× normal construction cost.
