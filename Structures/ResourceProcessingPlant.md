# Resource Processing Plant

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** Convert a defined resource stream into useful material

The plant is keyed to a specific resource/process. The organization understands: **this plant processes that resource.**

The plant requires input resource, processing equipment, power, qualified crew, maintenance and output storage/logistics.

If input is depleted, production does not magically continue. The facility enters a waiting/paused condition until the dependency becomes available.

The player can establish an intent such as **keep this resource stream moving**. The simulation handles the physical work.

Research may improve throughput, efficiency, purity, waste, reliability, energy consumption or supported processing methods.

Re-keying uses the Candidate rule of 1/4 normal construction time and 2× normal construction cost.
