# Barracks

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** Personnel accommodation and training capacity

A standard barracks contains four VR training modules, allowing one squad-sized training session at a time.

## Key

A barracks can be keyed to what it trains: basic soldier, construction crew, vehicle crew, aircraft crew, specialist weapons or advanced technical qualification. The exact curriculum is defined by the selected program.

## Boot camp

Basic soldiers pass through boot-camp VR training to become usable personnel. The player controls the **rate/intensity allocation** of training capacity rather than individually clicking every trainee.

## Bottleneck

More complicated capabilities require more qualified people. If a vehicle requires several squads, the organization needs enough barracks/training capacity to produce those squads.

## Re-keying

A barracks can be re-keyed to another training program. Candidate rule: 1/4 original construction time and 2× original construction cost.
