# VTOL Pad

**Design state:** Candidate

A VTOL pad is a physical operating capability for atmospheric vertical-launch and landing craft.

## It requires

- appropriate physical clearance;
- flight-control procedure;
- qualified flight crew;
- ground crew;
- servicing;
- communications;
- power/energy support;
- maintenance;
- safety procedures.

## Living world

The pad should show:

- aircraft arriving;
- crews moving;
- inspections;
- loading;
- servicing;
- launches;
- recoveries;
- emergency procedures.

The pad is not merely a place where the player clicks Launch.

## Strategic relationship

A transport operation depends on pad capacity, aircraft readiness, ground crews, loading, route planning and destination readiness.
