# Vehicle Factory

**Design state:** Candidate

A Vehicle Factory is a physical manufacturing capability keyed to a compatible vehicle family or production program.

## Requires

- factory structure;
- installed tooling;
- power;
- materials/components;
- qualified factory crew;
- quality control;
- maintenance;
- logistics;
- defined production procedure.

## Output

The factory creates actual vehicles.

Those vehicles have:

- configuration;
- production history;
- quality;
- condition;
- installed modules;
- ownership;
- crew requirements.

## Intent

The player can express:

> Build enough Carryalls to support the invasion.

The organization determines how many factories, crews, materials, shifts and logistics are available.

Production pauses when required dependencies are unavailable rather than creating vehicles from nothing.

## Re-keying

A compatible factory may be re-keyed to another production program using the current Candidate rule: one-quarter of original construction time and twice the normal construction cost. Tooling and staff still have to support the new production.
