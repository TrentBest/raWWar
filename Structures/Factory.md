# Factory

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** Physical production capability

A factory is keyed to what it produces: components, armor, weapons, electronics, sensors, propulsion, ground vehicles, aerospace vehicles, spacecraft, robotics or medical equipment.

## Factory crew

The factory itself requires qualified people: operators, technicians, engineers, quality control, maintenance, supervisors, logistics and safety/security as appropriate.

Building a factory does not create its workforce.

## Production reality

Production consumes real inputs. If required materials, components, power, tooling, qualified staff or storage are unavailable, production waits.

The player expresses production intent rather than micromanaging every transfer.

## Re-keying

A compatible factory can be re-keyed to a different production family. Candidate rule: 1/4 construction time and 2× normal construction cost. Re-keying does not create missing tooling or trained staff.
