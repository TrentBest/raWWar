# Recon VTOL

**Design state:** Candidate

A reconnaissance VTOL exists to create knowledge.

Its most important payload may be sensors rather than weapons.

## Capability

Candidate modules:

- high-resolution camera;
- thermal/alternate-spectrum sensors;
- radar;
- communications relay;
- electronic surveillance;
- mapping;
- stealth/low-observable package;
- small defensive weapon.

## Strategic effect

The vehicle may be the thing that makes an invasion possible.

The Commander cannot accurately plan the assault until someone establishes sight on enemy positions.

## FPS

A player can be the pilot, sensor operator, communications operator, or another qualified role.

The player sees what the actual installed sensors and communications chain permit.

Better technology therefore changes what the soldier literally sees.

## Loss

If the VTOL is destroyed, the information it gathered remains part of the appropriate knowledge/history state while the vehicle and surviving crew follow their own consequences.
