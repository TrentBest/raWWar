# Capital Ship

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** Large spacecraft / fleet warfare platform

A capital ship is a physical organization as much as a vehicle.

Possible spaces include bridge/command, engineering, weapons, sensors, communications, flight operations, maintenance, logistics, medical, crew quarters and launch/recovery facilities.

## FPS and RTS symmetry

From the Commander perspective, the ship is a strategic capability. From the FPS perspective, it is a place full of people performing procedures.

A player can potentially qualify for shipboard roles and work upward toward command of larger vessels.

## Fleet warfare

Capital ships participate in fleet battles. Damage affects actual systems and therefore the procedures the crew can perform. Consumables, ammunition, repairs, crew availability and replacement parts matter.

Destroyed ships may leave physical wreckage. Surviving personnel and casualties can remain part of the world rather than becoming an abstract unit count.

Exact ship hierarchy remains Illumination Needed.
