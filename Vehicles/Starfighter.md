# Starfighter

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** First-person atmospheric/orbital/space fighter capability

The Starfighter is the clearest bridge between raWWar's RTS Commander perspective and FPS Soldier perspective.

## Qualification

A player can qualify to fly a starfighter. Qualification is a career capability: train, qualify, receive assignment, prepare the craft, fly, fight, return, maintain and advance.

Candidate progression: Soldier → Fighter Pilot → Flight Lead → Squadron leadership → larger command responsibilities → capital-ship roles → fleet command. This is not yet a fixed rank tree.

## Physical reality

The fighter has finite ammunition, missiles, energy, fuel/propellant or equivalent, countermeasures and maintenance life.

A destroyed fighter does not automatically destroy the pilot's career if the pilot survives.

## Space combat

Fleet battles are part of the same game. The FPS player can participate from inside a spacecraft while the Commander sees fleet readiness, positions, logistics, orders and consequences.

Wreckage and dead soldiers/corpses may remain physically observable where the presentation and persistence rules support it.

The name raWWar means unfiltered war. Exact gore, corpse persistence and content boundaries remain Illumination Needed.
