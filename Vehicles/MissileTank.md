# Missile Tank

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** Ground-based missile warfare

A missile tank is explicitly a logistics-dependent weapons system.

## Core rule

**Missiles do not magically reappear.**

The vehicle carries a finite physical load, expends missiles, becomes depleted, and requires actual resupply through storage, transport, loading equipment, qualified personnel and procedure.

The player expresses intent rather than micromanaging every missile movement.

Example: **Maintain anti-air coverage for the base.** The organization seeks missiles, transport, loading and readiness. If the chain cannot satisfy the intent, coverage degrades or pauses.

## FPS experience

A qualified player may operate the vehicle, reload it, maintain it or crew its weapon systems.

## Research

Possible branches include missile guidance, sensor integration, launcher capacity, reload procedure, survivability, mobility and countermeasure resistance.
