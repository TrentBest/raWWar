# Armored Personnel Carrier

**Design state:** Candidate

An APC is a protected transport capability for moving infantry and equipment through dangerous territory.

## Configuration

Candidate modules:

- troop compartment;
- armor packages;
- communications;
- sensor package;
- remote weapon;
- medical conversion;
- command conversion;
- electronic-warfare conversion;
- cargo conversion.

The same chassis may therefore serve multiple organizations without pretending those configurations are identical.

## FPS

A soldier can ride inside it, operate a crew station, dismount into combat, reload it, maintain it, or command the vehicle.

## Commander

The Commander sees transport capacity, readiness, assigned squad, route, fuel/energy state, ammunition, damage, maintenance and destination.

The Commander should not have to manually tell each soldier when to open a hatch unless that detail becomes the meaningful interaction.

## Logistics

Transport capacity is finite. Damaged APCs need recovery and repair. Ammunition and other consumables are physical inventory.
