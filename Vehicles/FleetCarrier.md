# Fleet Carrier

**Design state:** Candidate

A Fleet Carrier is a large mobile military organization capable of carrying, launching, recovering, maintaining and supplying smaller spacecraft.

## It is a place

Candidate internal capabilities:

- flight deck;
- launch/recovery;
- fighter maintenance;
- ammunition storage;
- fuel/energy systems;
- sensor operations;
- command;
- communications;
- medical;
- crew accommodation;
- logistics;
- repair;
- pilot briefing and training.

## FPS

A player can live and work aboard the carrier.

They can walk through hangars, watch maintenance, qualify for flight operations, fly a fighter, return to the carrier, and see the consequences of fleet actions.

## Commander

The Commander sees the carrier as a strategic capability, but the ship remains populated by actual people performing the work.

## Fleet battle

A carrier's combat value depends on more than its weapon count. Aircraft availability, qualified pilots, deck crews, maintenance, ammunition, fuel/energy, repairs and communications all matter.

Exact carrier doctrine remains Illumination Needed.
