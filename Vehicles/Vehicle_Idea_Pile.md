## Things worth exploring

Some of the most interesting records may not be combat units.

A salvage ship recovering survivors from a destroyed fleet.

A mobile factory trying to repair a broken invasion supply chain.

A Carryall pilot deciding whether to enter an AA envelope.

A tanker trying to keep a fleet supplied.

A construction vehicle building a defensive line while the battle approaches.

A damaged spacecraft whose crew must abandon one compartment to keep another alive.

A small reconnaissance craft that discovers the enemy position and changes the entire invasion.

These are not yet mechanics. They are prompts for content design.
