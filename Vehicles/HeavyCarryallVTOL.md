# Heavy Carryall VTOL

**Design state:** Candidate  
**Role:** Strategic/tactical atmospheric transport

The Heavy Carryall transports soldiers, vehicles, equipment and supplies from prepared staging areas toward operational battlefields.

## Capability

Candidate configuration envelope: troop transport, vehicle cargo interface, external sling/load system, heavy cargo bay, armor, countermeasures, enhanced sensors, communications and rapid-deployment equipment.

Research can expand what the chassis can physically carry or survive.

## Battlefield procedure

stage → load → inspect → launch → transit → evade or survive AA risk → arrive → deploy → recover/return

The Commander can express intent such as: **Get the armored force to the western landing zone.** The simulation finds available Carryalls, crews, loading capacity, staging, routes, readiness and supplies.

## FPS experience

A qualified player physically operates the aircraft: preflight, loading coordination, takeoff, transit, threat response, landing/deployment and recovery.

## Upgrade space

Possible deterministic research branches include lift capacity, thruster performance, structural margin, cargo volume, deployment speed, armor, countermeasures, sensors, communications, energy efficiency and reliability.

Research unlocks legal configurations. Production builds them. Training makes them operable.

## Destruction and survival

If the Carryall is destroyed and its crew survives, those people remain people. They may become stranded personnel, rescue/recovery subjects, temporary reassignment, wounded survivors, or replacement crew for another qualified vehicle.

Exact survival, rescue and recovery procedures remain Illumination Needed.
