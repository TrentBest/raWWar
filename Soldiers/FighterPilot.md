# Fighter Pilot

**Design state:** Candidate

A Fighter Pilot is a soldier who has acquired the qualification necessary to operate a starfighter configuration.

Candidate progression: basic soldier → flight qualification → fighter qualification → operational pilot.

Further progression may lead toward flight leadership, squadron roles, command, advanced spacecraft qualifications and larger vessel operations. The exact progression is not yet frozen.

The player should actually fly: preflight, cockpit interaction, launch, flight, combat, navigation, communications, recovery and maintenance coordination.

A destroyed fighter is a vehicle loss. A surviving pilot remains a soldier. Their qualifications do not disappear merely because their previous aircraft did. They may be reassigned, rescued, retrained or promoted according to the authoritative world state.
