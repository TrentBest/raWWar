# raWWar Soldiers

This directory is the working laboratory for the people who make raWWar more than an RTS.

The Commander perspective and Soldier perspective are the **same simulation**.

A Commander sees readiness, organizations, staffing, logistics, research, production, fleets, bases and strategic consequences.

A soldier sees people, equipment, procedures, orders, training, work, danger, friendships, enemies, opportunities and the physical consequences of command decisions.

Neither view is a different game.

## Soldier records

Individual records should eventually explore identity, body and appearance, rank, qualifications, career, relationships, squad, equipment, responsibilities, routines, personality/variation, physical condition, history, future roles, FPS interactions and Commander relevance.

The goal is deliberately excessive detail.

**The soldiers are the stars.**
