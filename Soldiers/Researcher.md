# Researcher

**Design state:** Candidate

The Researcher works inside the physical research organization.

The researcher may:

- investigate;
- operate laboratory equipment;
- conduct experiments;
- record results;
- collaborate;
- prepare demonstrations;
- evaluate field experience;
- propose the next research program.

The Commander interacts with the administrator and research organization, but the researcher experiences the work itself.

A research program reaching 100 is a deterministic completion of that defined program. It does not randomly discover an unrelated technology.

Field experience can create new questions and therefore new defined research programs.
