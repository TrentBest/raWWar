# Vehicle Mechanic

**Design state:** Candidate

The Vehicle Mechanic is a soldier whose qualification turns damaged or unmaintained machinery back into capability.

## Work

Candidate procedures:

- inspect;
- diagnose;
- obtain parts;
- isolate equipment;
- repair;
- test;
- return to service.

## Commander perspective

The Commander sees fleet readiness and maintenance backlog.

The mechanic sees the actual broken machine.

## Gameplay

Repair should be physical work. The player can participate directly as a mechanic rather than merely pressing a repair button.

## Logistics

A mechanic cannot repair a missing component into existence. Parts must be produced, stored, transported and available.

This makes maintenance another expression of the same intent-driven logistics model.
