# Boot Camp Recruit

**Design state:** Candidate

The recruit is the beginning of the military capability pipeline.

person → boot camp → qualification → assignment → experience → further qualification

The recruit enters a barracks keyed to the appropriate training program. Four VR modules allow one squad-sized training session at a time.

The player controls training capacity and organizational priorities rather than individually producing every soldier.

Completion creates a qualified person with identity, basic military qualification, training history, assignment eligibility, equipment eligibility and an organizational record. The person is not an abstract population number.

The soldier deep dive should eventually describe physical training, instruction, simulation, failures, instructor interaction, squad formation, first assignment, equipment issue, qualification ceremony and first deployment.
